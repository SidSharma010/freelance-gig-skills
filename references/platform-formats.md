# Platform formats — reference

Shared specs so individual skills don't each restate this. All skills that
mention a platform should stay consistent with this file; update here first.

## Upwork

- **Format**: Cover letter attached to a bid, alongside a bid amount and
  estimated duration.
- **Length**: 150-300 words. First 2-3 lines are the preview before "see
  more" — they carry the most weight.
- **Connects**: Bids cost "Connects" (a paid credit). Boosting a bid costs
  extra connects — worth it mainly for well-matched, high-value jobs.
- **Fee structure**: Freelancer service fee scales down with lifetime
  billings per client (higher fee on a new client relationship, lower once
  you've billed more to that same client over time). Worth mentioning to
  the user when advising on long-term-client pricing strategy.
- **Client visibility**: Client sees your full profile, portfolio, and
  work history alongside the proposal — the letter should not repeat what's
  already visible there.

## Fiverr

- **Format**: Either a gig listing (buyer discovers you) or a response to a
  "Buyer Request" (you pitch them directly).
- **Buyer Request length**: 50-120 words — skimmed fast among many
  responses, so brevity and a clear outcome statement matter most.
- **Gig packages**: Basic / Standard / Premium tiers. Standard is usually
  the one most buyers pick — price and describe it as the "real" offer;
  Basic exists to catch price-sensitive buyers, not to be the main product.
- **Fee structure**: Flat commission on every transaction — factor this
  into gig pricing, since a listed price is not the take-home.

## Freelancer.com

- **Format**: A bid with a stated price and delivery timeframe, then the
  pitch — price-first is the platform norm, unlike Upwork/Fiverr.
- **Client base**: Skews toward agencies and repeat outsourcers more than
  Upwork/Fiverr's mix of individuals and small businesses.
- **Bidding dynamics**: Bids on open projects cluster low fast. Being the
  cheapest rarely wins alone — a specific, credible bid beats a bid that's
  merely cheap.
- **Milestones**: Common and expected for larger projects — breaking a
  deliverable into paid checkpoints reduces both sides' risk.

## Generic / unlisted platforms

Default to Upwork-style structure (hook → relevant proof → CTA) at
150-300 words unless the user describes a different norm for that specific
platform. Always confirm the platform if genuinely ambiguous.
